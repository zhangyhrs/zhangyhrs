## 👨‍💻 About Me

I work primarily in **natural resources survey and monitoring, intelligent remote sensing image interpretation, real estate and natural resources registration, geodesy, photogrammetry, and GIS**. I also develop reusable software tools, QGIS plugins, and automation workflows based on practical surveying and geospatial engineering needs.

I am also interested in **policies, regulations, technical standards, and industry specifications in the natural resources domain**, with a focus on their practical application in surveying, cadastral work, registration, remote sensing, and GIS production workflows.
