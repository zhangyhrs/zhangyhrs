<h2 id="about">👨‍💻 About Me</h2>

I work on **remote sensing, GIS, GeoAI, cadastral data processing, and natural resources survey & monitoring**. My development work focuses on turning real-world surveying, mapping, and geospatial workflows into reusable software tools, QGIS plugins, and automated processing solutions.

My current technical focus includes **remote sensing change detection and semantic segmentation, natural resources survey and monitoring, cadastral database processing, spatial data quality inspection, QGIS plugin development, and GIS automation**.

---

<h2 id="research">🧭 Research & Development Interests</h2>

- Remote Sensing & GeoAI
- High-resolution remote sensing image interpretation
- Semantic segmentation & change detection
- Pseudo-change suppression, boundary enhancement & multiscale feature fusion
- GIS automation & spatial data batch processing
- QGIS plugin development & open-source GIS tools
- Cadastral surveying, database processing & quality inspection
- Natural resources survey, monitoring & registration
- DEM-based terrain and geohazard analysis
