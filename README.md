## 👨‍💻 About Me

I work mainly in **natural resources survey and monitoring, intelligent remote sensing image interpretation, real estate and natural resources registration, geodesy, photogrammetry, and GIS**. I also focus on **policies, regulations, technical standards, and industry specifications in the natural resources domain**, and develop practical QGIS plugins and geospatial automation tools from real-world engineering workflows.
